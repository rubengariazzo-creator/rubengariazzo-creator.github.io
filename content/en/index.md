Engineering student at EPF, I design and build things: a rocket that actually flew, everyday objects reimagined (a telescopic watering can, a jewelry box), a drone rescue game, and independent research in physics and cryptanalysis shared in open access on Zenodo.

Driven by high-performance engineering and multidisciplinary technical projects, I'm particularly interested in Formula 1, motorsport, aerospace, and space systems. I like approaching complex problems from both a theoretical and practical angle, from modelling and code through to experimentation and data analysis. I'm expanding my computer science foundations with Harvard's CS50x (Introduction to Computer Science), and I'm a member of EPF Astronomie, my school's aerospace club.

This site gathers my finished projects, from the design process (specifications, CAD) to the final result.
