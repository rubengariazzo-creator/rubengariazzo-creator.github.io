Group business plan (Sonia, Auxence, Ruben, Ilyane) for **ReVolt**, a startup concept for electric retrofitting: converting combustion vehicles to electric instead of replacing them.

## Market

The French retrofit market is valued at €150-200M in 2024, growing 20-30% annually, driven by regulation (the 2035 ban on new combustion vehicles, low-emission zones) and public subsidies. A Porter's Five Forces analysis was used to shape the competitive strategy.

## Financials

- Projected 2026 revenue: €2.6M
- Projected net income: €560K
- 180 vehicles converted in the first year alone

Target customers: individuals and professional fleets, with full administrative support and subsidies deducted directly from the price.
