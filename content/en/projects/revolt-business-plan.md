Four of us (Sonia, Auxence, Ilyane and I) built this business plan and presented it on 12 May 2026. It is about **ReVolt**, a startup concept for electric retrofit, which means converting combustion-engine vehicles to electric instead of replacing them.

## The concept

ReVolt starts from the customer's own vehicle. A diagnosis first checks that it is compatible with retrofitting, then the combustion engine is replaced by an electric motor, with the battery and electronics it needs. The vehicle then gets its administrative update so it can legally run as an electric vehicle.

For each customer, a usage profile (daily mileage, driving areas, charging needs) helps pick the right range among three battery sizes, 170, 250 or 350 km, to avoid the weight and cost of an oversized battery. The offer targets private individuals, companies managing fleets and local authorities.

## Market

The French retrofit market was valued at 150-200 M EUR in 2024 (Avere-France), growing 20 to 30% a year, driven by regulation (the end of new combustion cars in 2035, low-emission zones) and public subsidies. About 60% of owners of older or professional vehicles are considering retrofitting.

We structured the strategy with Porter's five forces and a SWOT matrix. It shows a market that is still thinly occupied but growing fast, customers sensitive to price and reliability (hence extended warranties and transparent costs), and strong constraints such as kit homologation. The main threats are changing subsidies and how fast batteries are improving.

## The offer

We offer three all-inclusive packs for light and heavy utility vehicles, with a motor kit, a lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery for longevity, certified installation, UTAC homologation and a 2-year warranty.

Our first market is the Île-de-France region, where the Greater Paris low-emission zone pushes owners of combustion vehicles to find a solution, with two segments, collector vehicles and light utility vehicles. By combining State aid (a conversion bonus of up to 5,000 EUR, the ecological bonus, an interest-free loan) with aid from the Region and the City of Paris, more than half the cost can be covered. Thanks to subrogation, this aid is deducted straight from the invoice. The out-of-pocket cost starts at 4,442 EUR for a 170 km-range utility vehicle in the lowest-income households, and ReVolt keeps about 4,500 EUR of margin per vehicle whatever the aid.

## Figures

We forecast 2.6 M EUR of revenue in 2026, with 180 vehicles converted in the very first year. Variable costs (kits, batteries, insurance) come to 1.66 M EUR and fixed costs to 370 k EUR, including 174 k EUR of salaries and 140 k EUR of warehouse, which leaves a forecast profit of 560 k EUR.

The funding need is 315 k EUR (UTAC homologation, an initial stock of 15 kits, a 4-month salary reserve, workshop equipment). It would be covered by 100 k EUR of equity and 200 k EUR of debt, in the form of an SAS.
