Design of a jewelry box modeled in CATIA, developed across two successive iterations.

## V1, without a latch

First iteration: a cylindrical body and its lid, modeled and exported to STL for an initial 3D print.

## V2, with a latch

Second iteration, built as a 4-person group project: added a latch mechanism, with several rounds of dimensioning sketches, full-assembly CAD modeling, and prototyping (several central-cylinder sizes tested via PLA 3D printing). The latch (printed in blue on the prototypes) is designed to resist shock and torsion at its three load points: the bottom of the cylinder, the protruding handle, and between the drawers. A drop test, shown below, validates this shock resistance.
