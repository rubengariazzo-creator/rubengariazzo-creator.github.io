Finalist, at age 17, in the Grand Prix Poésie RATP with the poem *Danse sur les dunes* (in French).

## The contest

The Grand Prix Poésie RATP is France's largest amateur poetry competition, open to all ages, drawing several thousand submissions every year. *Danse sur les dunes* was selected among the 100 finalists of the 2025 edition.

## The publication

The poem was published in the anthology *Cent poèmes pour voyager – Finalistes du Grand Prix Poésie RATP 2025*, by Éditions Bruno Doucey (22 August 2025).
