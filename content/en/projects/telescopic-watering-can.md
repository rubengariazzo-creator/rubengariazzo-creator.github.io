Group project (6 people) designing a watering can that reaches hard-to-access plants (hanging pots, tall hedges, façades) without a ladder.

## The need

The functional specification targets individuals, renters, small shops, and people with reduced mobility: water up to more than 2.5 m high with a device that collapses down to 1.2 m for storage.

## Design process

Each subsystem (energy transfer, reservoir material, pump type) was compared through a Pugh matrix before locking in the technical choices. The working-principle diagrams then detail how the final design operates: a hollow-axle swivel joint for the hose reel, triangulation for stability, guide rings for the extending hose, and a piston-and-valve pump for pressurization.

## Chosen solution

- Telescopic metal mast, 2.5 m maximum extension
- 8 L reservoir (10.5 kg full, 2.5 kg empty)
- Integrated manual piston pump (0.3 bar, 1.5 L/min)
- Adjustable nozzle (jet / rain)
- Compact automatic hose reel and swivel front wheels for mobility
