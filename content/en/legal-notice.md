## Personal data

This site sets no non-essential cookies and collects no personal data through any form. It does use your browser's local storage (`localStorage`/`sessionStorage`) for two purely functional purposes, neither ever transmitted anywhere: remembering whether the homepage's one-time intro animation has already played during the current browsing session, and keeping score for an optional pixel-invader mini-game that appears while browsing. Neither stores any personal or identifying information, and both can be cleared at any time through your browser's own site-data settings. The contact methods offered are a mail link (`mailto:`), which opens your own email client, and a link to an external LinkedIn profile; no data is transmitted to or stored by the site itself via these links.
